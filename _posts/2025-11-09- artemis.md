Another not so great weekend, we had to let a cat go.  Artemis, was put down this afternoon and she will be missed greatly in my home.  I could say I have known and had her since she was a kitten and its just painful to see them go.  She went down hill fast and we could have spent a small fortune for possibly little gain.  Do we miss her, yes but sometimes this is the better choice. I hope she is at peace and is able to rest on the rainbow bridge in the sky with [Ty]().  May we meet again in someway.
